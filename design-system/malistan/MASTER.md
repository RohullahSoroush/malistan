# Malistan — visual direction

An editorial community archive grounded in real Malistan photography. Spacious composition, forest green (#234b3b), warm ivory (#f8f6f0), olive secondary text, warm brown accents. Georgia editorial headings paired with a fast-loading system sans serif. No external font request.

Use restrained borders, square image crops, small uppercase labels, generous section spacing, consistent Lucide outline icons and subtle image hover motion. A photographic hero introduces the place; distinct sections explore culture, photography, geography and community memory.

Design-system search returned Minimalism & Swiss Style with low-cost hover effects. Its generic enterprise palette was not suited to a community heritage archive, so the palette and editorial typography above are deliberate subject-specific adaptations. Accessibility rules remain applicable: visible keyboard focus, semantic navigation, native dialog, reduced motion, readable body text and responsive layouts.
